Building resilient supply chains for an uncertain world

By Priya Raman · May 14, 2026 · 5 min read

The past several years have made one thing clear: supply chain resilience is no longer a back-office concern. It is a strategic capability that protects revenue, reputation, and customer trust. The question facing leaders is not whether disruption will occur, but whether their networks are designed to absorb it.

From efficiency to adaptability

Decades of optimization produced lean, cost-efficient supply chains — but often at the expense of flexibility. The most resilient organizations are rebalancing, accepting modest cost trade-offs in exchange for the ability to respond quickly when conditions change.

Visibility is the foundation

You cannot manage what you cannot see. End-to-end visibility across suppliers, logistics, and inventory enables faster, better-informed decisions. Real-time data and predictive analytics turn surprises into manageable events.

Diversify deliberately

Concentration risk — in suppliers, geographies, or transport routes — is a silent threat. Thoughtful diversification, paired with strong supplier relationships, creates optionality without runaway complexity.

Plan for scenarios, not forecasts

Single-point forecasts inevitably miss. Scenario planning prepares teams to act decisively across a range of plausible futures, turning uncertainty into a source of competitive advantage.
